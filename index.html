Sock Drawer

The drawer takes socks two at a time. Only neighbors can trade places.

  1. The two socks closest to the drawer are the next pair in.
  2. Drag a sock sideways through its neighbor to swap them. A tap, then a tap on the sock beside it, does the same.
  3. When they match, tap the drawer to fold them in. If the timer runs out on a mismatch, it costs a life.

The line does not end. It speeds up, and new patterns join the laundry. Three mismatches and the drawer jams.
