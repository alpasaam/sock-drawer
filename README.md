# Sock Drawer

An endless laundry game. Socks ride a clothesline toward a drawer that takes them two at a time. You can only swap neighbors. Fold a matching pair in before the timer does it for you.

The line deals itself. Each new pair is scrambled, then repaired so a match is always a few neighbor-swaps from the drawer. More patterns join as your streak grows, and the timer gets shorter. Three mismatches jam the drawer.

## Play

- Drag a sock sideways through its neighbor to swap them.
- Or tap a sock, then tap the one beside it.
- The two socks closest to the drawer are next. When they match, tap the drawer to fold them in.
- If the timer runs out and they do not match, you lose a life.
- **P** or **Escape** pauses.

Your best score stays in the browser.
